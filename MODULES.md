# Modules

HollyEarth is one product with several worlds. The **City** is the place, **Politics**, **Sport** and **Social** are domains, **Cases** are the investigation layer that runs across them, and the **World** is another scale. Statuses are summarised here; details are in [docs/FEATURE_STATUS.md](docs/FEATURE_STATUS.md).

| Module | What it is | Status | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Start** | Orientation: what HollyEarth is, the five areas with maturity and data-origin badges, RU / LV / EN | `SHOWCASE_READY` | [README](README.md) |
| **City — Riga Twin** | Riga in 3D with district, council, sport, social and news layers; council panel; doors to the other worlds | `WORKING` | [docs/twin.md](docs/twin.md) |
| **Politics — Political Holly OS** | 12 modules on Riga City Council: Political OS, Network, Live Sessions, Decisions, AI Analyzer, City Budget, Deputies, Integral City, Parties & Power, District Impact, Smart City Lenses, Evidence Ledger | `BETA` (modules range from `WORKING` to `DEMO`) | [docs/politics.md](docs/politics.md) |
| **Political Workspace** | Internal research tool with 8 views over the canonical politics records | `PROTOTYPE` | [docs/politics.md](docs/politics.md#political-workspace) |
| **Sport — HollySport** | Sport City, clubs, athletes, events, venues, passports, archive, rankings, graph, map; Event OS, Creator and partnerships as prototypes; game layer as demo | `WORKING` (game layer `DEMO`) | [docs/sport.md](docs/sport.md) |
| **Social — Social Intelligence** | The registered social system of Riga as an interactive stage: catalogue, map, filters, dossiers, agreements, money, planning | `SHOWCASE_READY` | [docs/social.md](docs/social.md) |
| **Cases — Investigation** | Cases with claims, sources, quotations, case graph, typed money, open questions, roadmap, analysis, Story, monitoring and Holly | `WORKING` | [docs/cases.md](docs/cases.md) |
| **Monitoring** | Which official sources the cases watch, when they were checked, what changed | `BETA` | [docs/monitoring.md](docs/monitoring.md) |
| **Story** | A case told as chapters in Russian, Latvian or English, with optional voice | `WORKING` | [docs/story.md](docs/story.md) |
| **Holly** | Deterministic helpers, a case-scoped investigation assistant, optional model reasoning and voice | `BETA` | [docs/holly-ai.md](docs/holly-ai.md) |
| **World — Holly Eye** | Planetary view provided by a **third-party** open-source application, with a door back to the city | `BETA` (third-party integration) | [docs/holly-eye.md](docs/holly-eye.md) |
| **Research** | HEIO, HEIM and research influences (Integral / AQAL, Spiral Dynamics, Integral City) | `RESEARCH` | [docs/research.md](docs/research.md) |

Internal tools (project observatory, project brain, legacy demo modules) are not part of the product navigation. Concept modules that appear only as names in the module registry (for example practice, guide, academies) are **not** part of v0.1.0.
