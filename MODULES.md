# Modules

HollyEarth is organised as one core and several lenses on it. The navigation follows the same idea: the **City** is the place, three **domains** are lenses on it, and the **World** is another scale.

| Module | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Start** | Orientation page: what HollyEarth is, the five places, honest maturity and data-origin badges | SHOWCASE READY |
| **City — Riga Twin** | Riga in 3D with district, sport, social and council layers | WORKING |
| **Cases** | Investigations of the city: evidence, money, unknowns, roadmap, story | SHOWCASE READY |
| **Monitoring** | Which official sources the cases watch, when they were checked, what changed | BETA |
| **Politics** | Riga City Council as a graph, and the cases built on it | WORKING |
| **Sport — HollySport** | Clubs, athletes, events, venues; passports; HollySport City | WORKING (game layer DEMO) |
| **Social — Social Intelligence** | The registered social system of Riga as an interactive stage | SHOWCASE READY |
| **World — Holly Eye** | Planetary view, provided by a third-party open-source application, with a door back to the city | BETA (third-party) |

Concept modules that appear only as names (for example practice, guide, production, academies) are **not** part of v0.1.0.
