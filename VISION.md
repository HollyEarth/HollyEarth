# Vision

**Reality → Evidence → Intelligence → Experience**

- **Reality** — what institutions, registers, federations and archives actually publish.
- **Evidence** — those records kept with their sources, their status and their gaps.
- **Intelligence** — investigations that connect records across domains and say what is known, what is claimed and what is missing.
- **Experience** — a city a person can walk through: a 3D twin, a sport city, a map of services, a story told in their own language.

HollyEarth starts from one city, Riga, and three lenses — politics, sport and social services — over one graph. The idea is that any new domain becomes another lens on the same evidence, not another database, so that a citizen, an analyst and a journalist can all see the difference between what is registered, what is evidenced and what is merely asserted.
