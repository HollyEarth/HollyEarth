# Vision

## ONE SYSTEM. MANY WORLDS.

**Reality → Evidence → Intelligence → Experience → Action**

- **Reality** — what institutions, registers, federations and archives actually publish about a city: its places, organisations, people in public roles, decisions, money, services, sport and events.
- **Evidence** — those records kept with their sources, quotations, status and gaps, so that what is known, what is claimed and what is missing never blur together.
- **Intelligence** — investigations, analysis and open questions that connect records across domains.
- **Experience** — a city a person can walk through: a 3D twin, a sport city, a map of services, a case workspace, a story told in their own language.
- **Action** — in the future, governed action with appeals and recorded outcomes. Not part of v0.1.0.

The idea is that City, Politics, Sport, Social, Investigation and Intelligence become worlds of one system: one place, one organisation, one decision seen from every angle, and each new domain another world on the same evidence rather than another database. A citizen, an analyst and a journalist should all be able to see the difference between what is registered, what is evidenced, what is merely asserted, what is planned and what actually happened.

**The unknown is part of the model.**

## Vision is not today

HollyEarth v0.1.0 is one product over several connected domain models, not yet one unified reality graph. Politics and sport live in one canonical graph but are not yet linked; the social register has its own model; cases keep their own evidence graphs. What works today is listed in [CAPABILITIES.md](CAPABILITIES.md) and [docs/FEATURE_STATUS.md](docs/FEATURE_STATUS.md); how it is organised is in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md); where it goes next is in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

HollyEarth starts from one city, Riga.
