# Investigation cases

A **case** is a decision, a procurement or an organisation gathered together with its evidence, its money, what is still unknown and what to check next.

## What a case shows
- **What is established** — claims supported by a source, with how many are fully and partially supported.
- **What is unknown** — claims without direct support and the open research questions the case has not asked yet.
- **Money** — every sum with its type (planned, contract value, awarded, ceiling) and the rule that different types are not added together.
- **Analysis** — hypotheses and directions, labelled as such, with a plan of next steps.
- **Monitoring** — which official sources the case watches and whether they changed.

## Four ways to read a case
- **Explore** — the case as a network; select any object to see why it is here and where it came from.
- **Analyze** — the reasoning: hypotheses, what would confirm or refute them, and questions to Holly.
- **Roadmap** — the route the investigation actually took: which run opened which branch, and which questions remain.
- **Story** — the case told as a narrated sequence of chapters. See [story.md](story.md).

## Cases across domains
Cases exist in **politics** (for example council decisions on municipal companies and on delegating social work) and in **sport** (Riga combat-sport clubs). A sport club's passport and a social-service provider's dossier link to the case about them, where one exists.
