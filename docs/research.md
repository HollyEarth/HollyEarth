# Research foundations

HollyEarth separates what it has built from the ideas that guide it.

## Implemented in v0.1.0

- **An epistemic model** — source → snapshot → claim → evidence, with statuses and no unconditional "fact".
- **Provenance on every record.**
- **A canonical graph** for politics and sport records (social and cross-domain identity are in development — see [graph.md](graph.md)).
- **Spatial representation** — official district geometry, address-register points, a 3D city.

## Research — `RESEARCH`, not implemented as product features

### HEIO — HollyEarth Integral Ontology (v0.1)

<img src="../media/concepts/09-heio-concept-architecture.png" alt="HEIO concept illustration" width="100%">

<sub><b>Concept illustration, not a product screenshot.</b> Research concept architecture; not implemented as a complete system.</sub>

An internal research document (v0.1, July 2026) describing where a shared ontology for the city could go: what exists, how it is related, what is known about it and how it changes over time — from data, evidence and a reality graph to time and causality, perspectives, meaning, decisions, governed action and spatial experience. Some of its principles match v0.1.0 (provenance on everything, no unconditional fact); the ontology itself, its layers, actions and governance roles are not implemented.

### HEIM — HollyEarth Integrative Metatheory & Context System (v0.2)

<img src="../media/concepts/10-heim-context-system.png" alt="HEIM concept illustration" width="100%">

<sub><b>Concept illustration, not a product screenshot.</b> Research concept architecture; not implemented in v0.1.0.</sub>

An internal research document (v0.2, July 2026) about **how to ask complex questions**: framing the question and its context, choosing lenses, keeping competing explanations side by side, being explicit about methods, evidence and uncertainty, and keeping the inquiry open. HEIM is meant to sit beside HEIO — HEIO describes what exists, HEIM how to investigate it. It is not implemented in v0.1.0.

### Research influences

| Influence | How it is used |
|---|---|
| Integral theory / AQAL | Research lenses (for example a quadrant view in the internal Political Workspace and a Twin overlay); never a finding |
| Spiral Dynamics | Considered only as context for places and processes — **never used to rate people, parties or districts** |
| Integral City | Design influence; the related Politics module has no data in v0.1.0 |
| Systems thinking, urban and spatial studies, network science, open knowledge, responsible AI | Design influences |

These frameworks are design lenses, not scientific claims. HollyEarth does not claim that any of them is scientifically validated, and it does not score or rank people with them.
