# Social — Social Intelligence

Social Intelligence is the social-services lens: Riga's registered social system as a place you can explore.

## What it holds — WORKING
- The Ministry of Welfare **register of social service providers**, filtered to services delivered in Riga: who provides each service, for whom, where, how it is delivered (at a location or at home), and the client capacity planned at registration.
- Delivery buildings confirmed by the **State Address Register**; services delivered at home have no point on the map, and the legend says so.
- **Agreements** from the municipality's list of delegation and cooperation agreements, matched to providers with the strength of each match shown.
- **Money** read from agreements — planned amounts by year and per-client rates — each with its type; rates are never summed.
- A **planning** view of how registered services are distributed, labelled **not a fact**.

## The stage — SHOWCASE READY
- Riga as the interface: choose a type of service and the city shows where it is.
- Filters for target group, district and delivery mode, and a search across services, organisations and places.
- **"Holly sees"** — counts the register itself computes (for example organisations running several services), with the reminder that they say nothing about need.
- **Dossiers** for services and providers, opening beside the map; a provider links to an investigation case where one exists.
- Agreements, money and planning open from the stage.
- Russian, Latvian and English; a dedicated phone layout.

## Registered ≠ received
The register tells HollyEarth what is registered. It does not tell how many people actually received a service, whether it is running today, what was actually paid, or how good it was — and HollyEarth shows those as **unknown**. The catalogue does not decide whether a person is entitled to a service.

Need, outcomes and actual payments: **RESEARCH**.
