# Riga Twin

The **City** in HollyEarth is a 3D twin of Riga: a photogrammetric city scene with layers from the three lenses.

## Layers
- **Districts** — Riga's neighbourhoods from official boundaries.
- **Sport** — plots granted to sport institutions; clubs and venues with confirmed points.
- **Social** — buildings where registered social services are delivered, confirmed by the State Address Register.
- **Council** — council decisions that name an address, pinned on the map; the council network (factions, deputies, committees, organisations).
- **News** — district headlines with links (no article text stored).

## Honest by design
- Layers with no data say **"no data"** instead of disappearing.
- Illustrative district scores are labelled **DEMO**.
- A council decision placed by an address in its title is marked as an **inference**, not a fact.

## Holly Eye
From the Twin, a door leads to the planetary **Holly Eye** view and back. Holly Eye is a **third-party integration** of the open-source God's Eye View application; it is not HollyEarth's own technology. Status: **BETA**.
