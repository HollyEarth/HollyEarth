## How the public copies are made

- The application frame is kept **unchanged** — including its own map credits and the map provider's notices (for example "Upgrade for commercial use"). Nothing is removed, covered or redrawn.
- An information band is **added below** the frame: the status of the feature, the integration credit, the layer-data source and the map / imagery credit.
- For OpenStreetMap frames the application shows only a "Data attribution" link inside the frame; the band adds "© OpenStreetMap contributors" next to the image.

All 46 screenshots are published on that basis. If the use of this repository becomes commercial or promotional, the 36 Google 3D frames and the OpenSky frames must be reviewed again and replaced or removed.
