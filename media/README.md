# Media index

Every image published in this repository: file, title, status shown, attribution and any change made in the public copy. Browse them by world in the **[HollyEarth Visual Atlas](../docs/GALLERY.md)**. Publication rules: [SCREENSHOT_PLAN.md](SCREENSHOT_PLAN.md).

**131 images** — 18 screenshots (PNG), 102 gallery screenshots (WebP), 2 research concept illustrations (PNG), 9 marked concept illustrations (WebP).

## About these images

- **Screenshots** are captures of the real HollyEarth v0.1.0 product, October 2026, 1920×1080, captured with the database read-only and paid services switched off. Many are in the Russian interface; English versions will follow. Source data stays in its original language.
- **Public copies.** Gallery images are WebP copies of the originals. A copy is changed only where the change is listed in the last column — a blur over personal data, or a visible badge/correction. Originals are kept unchanged outside this repository.
- **Statuses** follow [docs/FEATURE_STATUS.md](../docs/FEATURE_STATUS.md); `INTERNAL` marks the project's own control-plane screens.
- **Product illustrations** inside screenshots (HollySport posters, fighter figures, "atmosphere" backgrounds, Creator visuals) are illustration in the product, not photographs of real events, clubs or people.
- **Concept illustrations** are not product screenshots. They are AI-generated design explorations; every copy carries a banner, and those with invented data say FICTIONAL EXAMPLE DATA.

## Attribution

- **3D city (Riga Twin, HollySport City):** GeoRiga photorealistic 3D model of Riga and LOD2 buildings; Latvian Geospatial Information Agency (LGIA) reality model; ArcGIS platform — "Powered by Esri"; OpenStreetMap contributors (CC BY-SA); mesh generated with Agisoft Metashape. The product shows these attributions on the map.
- **Neighbourhood boundaries:** data.gov.lv (Riga apkaimes). **Building points:** State Address Register, Valsts zemes dienests (VZD).
- **Social services:** Sociālo pakalpojumu sniedzēju reģistrs, Labklājības ministrija (snapshot 2026-09-14).
- **Politics:** Riga City Council publications and voting protocols (riga.lv, pkip.riga.lv); Central Election Commission; municipal spending dataset (data.gov.lv). **Deputy portraits** are the official portraits from the Riga City Council's public pages.
- **Sport:** athletics.lv, WAKO official results books, the King of Kings event archive, the register of recognised federations (LSFP), the HollySport research dataset of combat-sport clubs. An athlete photo shown in a frame carries its own credit line.
- **Cases and monitoring:** Register of Enterprises (UR), Electronic Procurement System (EIS) via data.gov.lv, and official documents cited in each case.

Full list of sources and the state of their reuse terms: [DATA_SOURCES.md](../DATA_SOURCES.md).
