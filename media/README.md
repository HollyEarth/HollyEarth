# Media index

Every image published in this repository, with its caption, status and attribution. Publication rules: [SCREENSHOT_PLAN.md](SCREENSHOT_PLAN.md).

- **Screenshots** are unedited captures of the real HollyEarth v0.1.0 product (no retouching, cropping or blurring; safe states were reached only through the interface). Captured October 2026 at 1920×1080 from the v0.1.0 baseline, with the database opened read-only and paid services switched off. Some screens are in the Russian interface; source data stays in its original language.
- **Concept illustrations** explain ideas; they are **not** product screenshots and are always captioned as such.
- Product art visible inside screenshots (HollySport posters, "atmosphere" backgrounds) is illustration in the product, not photographs of real events, clubs or people.
- **Not yet published:** screenshots of the 3D city (Riga Twin, HollySport City), until the reuse terms of the Riga 3D city model are confirmed.
