## Real names and faces — publication rules

Not to be shown, or shown only blurred:
- **Private individuals**: athletes (including adult competitors in KOK and WAKO archives), coaches, club staff, people named in investigation documents who do not hold public office.
- **Faces** of anyone, including portraits of public officials taken from institutional websites — image rights have not been reviewed.
- **Addresses of sensitive social services**: crisis centres, shelters, night shelters, services for victims of violence or trafficking, services for people with addictions.
- **Test or internal accounts**, email addresses, local URLs, ports, file paths.

Acceptable with care:
- Names of public institutions, municipal companies, registered organisations and federations.
- Names of elected officials in their official role, as published by the council — but prefer views where the network, not a person, is the subject.

Before publication each screenshot gets a one-line review note: date captured, release, what was blurred, who approved it.
