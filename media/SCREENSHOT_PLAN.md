# Screenshot rules — v0.1.0

The published screenshots and their captions are listed in [README.md](README.md). This page holds the rules every screenshot and video frame must pass before it is published.

## Capture

- Captured from the v0.1.0 baseline with the database opened read-only and paid services switched off.
- 1920×1080 (owner decision, October 2026); interface language stated in the caption.
- Unedited: no retouching, redrawing, cropping or blurring. Safe states are reached only through the interface. A frame that would need editing is not published.
- No browser address bar, developer tools, terminal, file paths, ports, test accounts or server errors in frame.
- DEMO, CONCEPT and PREVIEW labels stay in frame and are repeated in the caption.

## Real names and faces — not shown

- **Private individuals**: athletes (including adult competitors), coaches, club staff, and people named in investigation documents who do not hold public office.
- **Minors**, in any form.
- **Faces** of anyone, including portraits of public officials taken from institutional websites — image rights have not been reviewed.
- **Addresses of sensitive social services**: crisis centres, shelters, night shelters, services for victims of violence or trafficking, services for people with addictions.

## Data honesty — not shown

- Labels that overstate certainty (for example a "verified" badge on unverified records).
- Raw model output presented as fact, or internal status codes without explanation.
- Screens with known false or contradictory figures, unless the caption states the limitation.
- Concept illustrations without the caption "concept illustration, not a product screenshot", or concept images that state something untrue about v0.1.0.

## Acceptable with care

- Names of public institutions, municipal companies, registered organisations and federations.
- Official election results and aggregate register counts.

Each published screenshot carries a review note in the media index; requests to remove a person, address or other detail are handled promptly (see [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md)).
