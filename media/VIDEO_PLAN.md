## Production rules
- Recorded at 1440×900 or 1920×1080, interface language stated in the caption.
- No browser address bar, developer tools, terminal, file paths or ports in frame.
- No narration with a synthetic voice that imitates a real person.
- Captions mark any DEMO or PROTOTYPE surface shown.
- Each clip gets a review note (date, release, what was blurred, who approved it).
