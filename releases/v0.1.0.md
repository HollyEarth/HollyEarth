# HollyEarth v0.1.0 — Public Baseline

**One evidence core. Three lenses. One city.**

HollyEarth v0.1.0 is the first integrated baseline of an evidence-aware spatial intelligence platform for Riga. It brings council records, sport records and the social-services register into one graph, keeps the source and status of every record, and turns questions about the city into investigation cases that can be explored on a 3D twin and told as a story.

## Highlights
- **Investigation cases** with evidence, typed money, open questions, roadmap and story — SHOWCASE READY.
- **Social Intelligence** — Riga's registered social system as an interactive stage — SHOWCASE READY.
- **HollySport** — real sport data and passports for clubs, athletes, events and venues — WORKING.
- **3D Riga Twin** — WORKING.
- **Monitoring** of the sources behind cases — BETA.

## Honest limits
- Runs as a private installation; no public hosted instance yet.
- No general Holly assistant outside cases.
- HollySport's season, missions and XP are a DEMO preview.
- Social intelligence covers registered supply, not need, outcomes or actual payments.
- The planetary view is a third-party integration.

## Correction (2026-10-09)

The description above says v0.1.0 brings council records, sport records and the social-services register "into one graph". That is not accurate: politics and sport records share the canonical graph (as two sub-graphs not yet linked to each other), while the social-services register keeps its own model, and its 177 buildings are not canonical places. The case workspace is now rated `WORKING` rather than showcase-ready because of known money-display inconsistencies.
